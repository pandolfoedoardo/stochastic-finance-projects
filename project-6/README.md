This project implements Monte Carlo simulation methods to evaluate derivatives under the Black-Scholes model. It covers Geometric Brownian Motion trajectory generation, comparing the discretization error of the Euler-Maruyama scheme against exact multi-step and single-step formulations. Pricing algorithms are implemented for path-dependent contracts—specifically Asian, Lookback, and Barrier options—and validated against closed-form benchmarks such as the Kemna-Vorst formula. Finally, the framework extends to a multi-asset setting to price a 'Worst-Of Cash Collect' certificate on three European equities (LVMH, Pernod Ricard, Hermès) using a Cholesky decomposition to handle asset correlation alongside discrete observation dates and autocall barriers
