This study focuses on the pricing and risk analysis of European Down-and-Out barrier options. We evaluate pricing dynamics through both closed-form formulations—validating the fundamental In-Out parity—and Monte Carlo simulations to quantify the impact of discretization bias. Particular attention is devoted to hedging challenges by analyzing the severe instability of the Greeks, such as the sharp Delta drop and Gamma explosion near the barrier. Finally, we investigate numerical limitations in extracting implied volatility for path-dependent derivatives, examining potential failure modes and convergence issues inherent to standard root-finding algorithms.
