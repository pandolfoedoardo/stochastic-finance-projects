This project explores various methodologies for calculating Value at Risk (VaR) applied to a portfolio consisting of two uncorrelated assets: IBIT (Bitcoin ETF) and LVMH. I implemented and compared parametric approaches (Sigma Flat, EWMA), Monte Carlo simulations, and historical methods (Historical VaR and Simulation). The analysis mathematically demonstrates the benefits of diversification (subadditivity) resulting from the negative correlation between the two assets. Furthermore, the results highlight the limitations of traditional Gaussian distributions in capturing "fat tails" and the true risk profile of highly volatile assets like Bitcoin.
